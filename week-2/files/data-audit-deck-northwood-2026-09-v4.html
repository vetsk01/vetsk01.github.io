04How the score works

Eight weighted dimensions. Three readiness bands.

Each portco is scored 0–4 on eight dimensions, then combined into a composite out of 100. This is the methodology behind every number in this deck — reusable next quarter without Kith.

0–39

Not diligence-ready

Foundational gaps; the baseline cannot yet be relied on in diligence. All ten portcos sit here today.

40–74

Partially ready

A usable baseline exists, with specific, namable gaps.

75–100

Diligence-ready

Supports LP disclosure or buyer diligence with minor polish.

The ceiling today is about 63: dimensions 3–6 carry 40% of the weight, and no portco scores above 1 on any of them. Sterling and Meridian sit 2.5 points from Partially ready on unrounded composites — treat the band as a close call and the dimension profile as the more reliable read. The weights were fixed on September 22, 2026, after the first automated checks had run, so with the data in view. They are held unchanged until request responses arrive, so they can't be tuned to results.
