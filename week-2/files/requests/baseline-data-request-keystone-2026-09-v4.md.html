Baseline Climate Data Request: Keystone Behavioral Health

From: Sarah Mitchell, COO, Northwood Capital (prepared with Kith Consulting)
To: Jennifer Martinez (COO) and William Park (CFO)
Issue date: Monday, September 28, 2026 (planned)
Edition: Portfolio edition: Kith Climate cohort case, fictional client. The issue timetable is as planned in September 2026.
Status: As prepared for issue, September 2026

Why This Matters

This is Keystone's first emissions baseline. Northwood will use it in Keystone's board materials, and it is the starting point for the energy and site-selection questions raised in your questionnaire. Your submission noted it was prepared without CFO review and offered corrections; this request is the opportunity for that review.

What We Found in Your FY 2025 Submissions

We compared your carbon collection template with your climate questionnaire, figure by figure.

  • The carbon template totals 1,312,000 kWh of electricity and 114,800 therms of gas. The questionnaire reports about 1,494,000 kWh and 120,400 therms: 12.2% and 4.7% higher.
  • Six clinics carry an identical square-footage estimate (33,000 kWh each) in place of utility data.
  • Trenton and Hagerstown opened in 2025 and are partial-year; Hagerstown has no data yet.
  • The template includes Richmond VA with FY 2025 utility figures (76,000 kWh and 4,200 therms); your FY 2026 budget lists Richmond as a clinic opening in Q3 2026.

What You Have Already Provided

These are not requested again:

  • Electricity and gas for 21 locations, with the source for each.
  • Confirmation that Keystone has no fleet vehicles or on-site fuel use.
  • Weather-related closures in 2025 and their effect on sessions.

How We Will Use It

  • Your data is used for your company's baseline and Northwood's reporting only. It is not shared with other portfolio companies.
  • Where an item hasn't arrived by the time the baseline is built, we use your submitted figures, mark them as provisional, and note what was assumed.
  • If a document isn't available in the form described, send what you have and tell us what it covers. A partial answer by the date is more useful than a complete one later.

How to Respond

Reply to Sarah Mitchell, quoting the reference (for example, KBH-1) against each item. Files can be sent in any format. Questions on any item can go to Daniel Vetsko at Kith Consulting, who is preparing the baseline.
