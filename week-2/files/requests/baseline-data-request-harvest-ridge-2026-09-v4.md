## Why This Matters

Three of your top ten customers, about $34.0M of combined revenue, now require Scope 3 supplier emissions data as a condition of 2027 contract renewal, and your strategic plan expects that to spread. Those customers will need Harvest Ridge's own footprint first, and it has to be right the first time: a baseline built on the wrong figures would have to be restated in front of them.

## What We Found in Your FY 2025 Submissions

We compared your carbon collection template with your climate questionnaire, figure by figure.

- Harvest Ridge submitted FY 2025 energy data twice, in the carbon template and the climate questionnaire, and the two disagree on every total: electricity -14.6%, gas -18.7%, on-site diesel +88.2%, propane -35.5%, fleet diesel -62.2% and fleet gasoline +9.1%.
- The questionnaire states its figures are April to December after the acquisition, annualized. The template doesn't state a period.
- Three template rows (Murfreesboro, Bowling Green, Chattanooga) have an extra field, which pushes the source label and notes out of position.
- The template has no field for R-404A or R-134a, so the refrigerant systems described in your questionnaire aren't captured.

## What You Have Already Provided

- Your supplier file (20 suppliers). We are asking only for sourcing evidence behind it, not a new supplier-spend submission.
- Refrigerant system descriptions and the June 2025 Nashville leak repair, from the questionnaire.
