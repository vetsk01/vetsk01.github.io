Baseline Climate Data Request: Sterling Precision Manufacturing

From: Sarah Mitchell, COO, Northwood Capital (prepared with Kith Consulting)
To: Janet Chen (CFO) and Derek Williams (COO)
Issue date: Monday, September 28, 2026 (planned)
Edition: Portfolio edition: Kith Climate cohort case, fictional client. The issue timetable is as planned in September 2026.
Status: As prepared for issue, September 2026

Why This Matters

Your questionnaire identifies Raytheon, L3Harris and Boeing Defense as customers asking for supplier carbon data, with Boeing Defense making it mandatory from 2027, across $25.0M to $42.0M of RFP opportunities. Defense primes assess this data at facility level and against source documents. This request is about making Sterling's figures hold up to that review, and building the baseline Northwood will use for Sterling's board materials.

What We Found in Your FY 2025 Submissions

We compared your carbon collection template with your climate questionnaire, figure by figure.

  • Your two FY 2025 submissions (the carbon template and the climate questionnaire) agree exactly on every company total. That is a good starting point.
  • At facility level they differ for all three plants, on both electricity (-13.7% to +34.0%) and gas (-24.0% to +68.1%). On-site diesel and propane are also allocated to different plants in the two submissions.
  • The template records R-410A at Elyria only (15 lbs); the questionnaire reports 45 lbs across the three plants.
  • The template lists three facilities (Akron, Elyria, Canton). Your add-on records place MicroTech Tooling in Wisconsin, which the template does not show.
  • The template describes the Akron R-22 chillers as scheduled for replacement in 2026. The questionnaire describes the replacement as deferred and still being requested.

What You Have Already Provided

These are not requested again:

  • Company totals for electricity, gas, on-site fuel and fleet fuel, which reconcile across both submissions.
  • Fleet fuel volumes (about 12,000 gallons of diesel and 4,500 gallons of gasoline). We are asking only for the receipts behind them, not new figures.
  • Your supplier list (15 suppliers). We are asking only for sourcing documentation behind it.

How We Will Use It

  • Your data is used for your company's baseline and Northwood's reporting only. It is not shared with other portfolio companies.
  • Where an item hasn't arrived by the time the baseline is built, we use your submitted figures, mark them as provisional, and note what was assumed.
  • If a document isn't available in the form described, send what you have and tell us what it covers. A partial answer by the date is more useful than a complete one later.

How to Respond

Reply to Sarah Mitchell, quoting the reference (for example, SPM-1) against each item. Files can be sent in any format. Questions on any item can go to Daniel Vetsko at Kith Consulting, who is preparing the baseline.
