Summary

This request covers two things. First, the items only Northwood can answer or decide, two of which are needed before the portco requests go out. Second, the plan for issuing the portco requests on Monday, September 28, 2026, so that the FY 2025 baseline can be built in Week 3 with as much confirmed data as possible.

Coverage. The engagement priced four priority portcos for this stage. We have extended the audit and the data requests to all ten active portcos at no additional cost. The process is scripted and documented, so applying it evenly across the portfolio costs little more than applying it to four. The same framework re-runs each quarter, and extends to further portcos, at no added cost. Whether the six additional requests (Keystone, NovaCare, Patriot, Pinnacle, Ridgeline and Trident) go out on Monday or later is your decision.

Every ask across the eleven requests sits in one of three tiers, set by a single rule:

TierTestDue

If Answers Are Late

The Week 3 baseline still covers all 10 companies, on a stated provisional rule:

  • Where the template is known to be wrong and the questionnaire gives a figure (for example NovaCare's two migrated clinics, Patriot's two zeroed branches and Meridian's four zero-gas sites), the questionnaire figure is used and flagged.
  • Where the two submissions conflict and neither is known to be right, as at Harvest Ridge, a range is shown.
  • Everything else uses the submitted template, marked provisional.

Each such figure carries a note stating what was assumed and what would close the gap. Late Tier 1 answers reduce confidence in specific figures, but they don't stop the baseline.
