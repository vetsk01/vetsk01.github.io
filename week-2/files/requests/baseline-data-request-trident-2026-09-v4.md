## Why This Matters

Your questionnaire notes that healthcare and financial services clients run ESG questionnaires in vendor review, and Trident has already calculated a footprint for this submission. The baseline Northwood builds should be consistent with the figure Trident gives clients, so we'd like to understand the method behind it.

## What We Found in Your FY 2025 Submissions

We compared your carbon collection template with your climate questionnaire, figure by figure.

- Gas matches exactly between the carbon template and the questionnaire. Electricity differs by 4.1% (1,079,500 kWh in the template, about 1,126,000 kWh in the questionnaire).
- Six of eight facilities are sourced from accounting records, with no methodology note.
- Your organisation chart lists Midwest HQ, Charlotte, Chicago (ShieldPoint SOC) and remote staff. The template lists eight offices, puts the SOC in Cleveland, and has no Chicago row; five of its offices are not in the organisation chart's location table.
- Trident's calculated footprint is about 595 metric tons CO2e (95 Scope 1, 500 Scope 2). Applying the standard EPA factor to the reported 50,000 therms of gas alone gives about 265 metric tons, so we'd like to check the method with you.
