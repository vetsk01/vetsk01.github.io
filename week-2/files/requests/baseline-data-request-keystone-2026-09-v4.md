## Why This Matters

This is Keystone's first emissions baseline. Northwood will use it in Keystone's board materials, and it is the starting point for the energy and site-selection questions raised in your questionnaire. Your submission noted it was prepared without CFO review and offered corrections; this request is the opportunity for that review.

## What We Found in Your FY 2025 Submissions

We compared your carbon collection template with your climate questionnaire, figure by figure.

- The carbon template totals 1,312,000 kWh of electricity and 114,800 therms of gas. The questionnaire reports about 1,494,000 kWh and 120,400 therms: 12.2% and 4.7% higher.
- Six clinics carry an identical square-footage estimate (33,000 kWh each) in place of utility data.
- Trenton and Hagerstown opened in 2025 and are partial-year; Hagerstown has no data yet.
- The template includes Richmond VA with FY 2025 utility figures (76,000 kWh and 4,200 therms); your FY 2026 budget lists Richmond as a clinic opening in Q3 2026.
