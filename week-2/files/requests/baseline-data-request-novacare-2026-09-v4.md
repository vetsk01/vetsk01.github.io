## Why This Matters

Northwood is building an FY 2025 emissions baseline for each portfolio company, for board materials and Northwood's own reporting. A small number of entries in NovaCare's submission would materially change NovaCare's figures if left as they are, so we'd like to correct them before the baseline is built.

## What We Found in Your FY 2025 Submissions

We compared your carbon collection template with your climate questionnaire, figure by figure.

- Marietta and Augusta (labelled "RehabWorks acquisition" in the template, data migrated from the prior owner's format) show zero electricity and 82,000 and 74,000 therms of gas. These gas figures are more than ten times a typical NovaCare clinic's, and together they put the template's gas total 90.5% above the questionnaire's total. The values may have been entered in the wrong field. Neither city appears among the 28 clinics in your organisation chart.
- The template lists 30 sites. 12 match clinics in your organisation chart (28 clinics) and 16 do not. Harrisburg and Virginia Beach carry FY 2025 figures, but your 2026 plans describe them as clinics opening in 2026.
- Baltimore reports 312,000 kWh of electricity, about 3.5 times the typical NovaCare clinic, with no note explaining it.

## What You Have Already Provided

These are not requested again:

- Electricity and gas figures for the other 27 sites, and the split between direct utility accounts and landlord-supplied sites. Which sites operated in FY 2025 is confirmed in the request below.
- Confirmation that NovaCare operates no vehicle fleet.
