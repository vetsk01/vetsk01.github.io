Why This Matters

Your questionnaire notes refinery clients with about $18.0M of combined annual revenue now sending ESG questionnaires as part of contractor qualification. A clean, calendar-year baseline is the basis for answering them credibly, and for the board materials Northwood prepares for Pinnacle.

What We Found in Your FY 2025 Submissions

We compared your carbon collection template with your climate questionnaire, figure by figure.

  • All four energy and fuel totals match exactly between the carbon template and the questionnaire.
  • The questionnaire lists 9 facilities and does not include Port Arthur; the template has 10, including Port Arthur.
  • The questionnaire describes electricity as coming from utility bills. The template labels 8 of 10 facilities as accounting records.
  • The questionnaire figures are 12-month trailing averages to November 30, 2025, and fleet diesel includes generator fuel.

What You Have Already Provided

These are not requested again:

  • Company totals for electricity, gas, fleet diesel and fleet gasoline, consistent across both submissions.
  • Insurance premium history and the broker's 2026 renewal expectation.
  • Weather disruption history with revenue impact.

How We Will Use It

  • Your data is used for your company's baseline and Northwood's reporting only. It is not shared with other portfolio companies.
  • Where an item hasn't arrived by the time the baseline is built, we use your submitted figures, mark them as provisional, and note what was assumed.
  • If a document isn't available in the form described, send what you have and tell us what it covers. A partial answer by the date is more useful than a complete one later.
