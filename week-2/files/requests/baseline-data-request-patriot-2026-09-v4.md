## Why This Matters

Your questionnaire makes a clear link between heat exposure and workers' compensation costs, and notes client questions about temporary-worker transport. Fleet fuel is also likely to be the largest single item in Patriot's own footprint. Getting these figures right matters for the baseline Northwood builds for Patriot, and for the fleet decision tied to the fleet management contract renewal your questionnaire expected in Q3 2026.

## What We Found in Your FY 2025 Submissions

We compared your carbon collection template with your climate questionnaire, figure by figure.

- Your questionnaire tables give facility figures for all 26 locations. 24 match the carbon template exactly.
- The template and questionnaire list 26 locations, but your organisation chart lists 24 branches. 14 of the 26 match; 12 do not, and 10 branches in the organisation chart are not in the template.
- Evansville and Rockford are zero in the template ('utility costs included in lease') but have figures in the questionnaire (36,000 and 40,000 kWh; 3,200 and 4,200 therms).
- The questionnaire attributes 76,700 gallons of gasoline to 8 branch-manager vehicles, about 9,600 gallons per vehicle, which is well above typical use. The template spreads fleet fuel across all 26 locations.
- The template notes Louisville as closed in August 2025 with a full-year estimate; the questionnaire lists it as an active branch.
