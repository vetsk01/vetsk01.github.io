Baseline Climate Data Request: Ridgeline Property Services

From: Sarah Mitchell, COO, Northwood Capital (prepared with Kith Consulting)
To: Christine Albright (CFO)
Issue date: Monday, September 28, 2026 (planned)
Edition: Portfolio edition: Kith Climate cohort case, fictional client. The issue timetable is as planned in September 2026.
Status: As prepared for issue, September 2026

Why This Matters

Your questionnaire notes a REIT client (about $4.5M of revenue) that intends to require property-manager emissions reporting by 2027, and your board is evaluating a sustainability services line. Both start with Ridgeline being able to state its own footprint accurately. At the moment the carbon template captures only part of it.

What We Found in Your FY 2025 Submissions

We compared your carbon collection template with your climate questionnaire, figure by figure.

  • Three template rows (Denver HQ, Phoenix, Salt Lake City) have an extra field, which pushes the fleet gasoline figure into the source column.
  • Denver HQ gas is 8,500 therms in the template and 4,200 therms in the questionnaire. Phoenix electricity is 95,000 kWh in the template and about 62,000 kWh in the questionnaire.
  • The Las Vegas office (opened January 2025) isn't in the template.
  • The template holds about 10,600 gallons of fleet gasoline and no diesel; the questionnaire reports 22,400 gallons of gasoline and 4,800 gallons of diesel for 40 vehicles.
  • Regional office electricity is labelled as utility bills in the template, while the questionnaire describes it as estimated from square footage.

What You Have Already Provided

These are not requested again:

  • Summary energy data for the managed portfolio. Whether managed buildings belong in Ridgeline's own footprint is a separate decision Northwood will take. No managed-portfolio data is requested now.
  • Insurance premium history and weather incident history.

How We Will Use It

  • Your data is used for your company's baseline and Northwood's reporting only. It is not shared with other portfolio companies.
  • Where an item hasn't arrived by the time the baseline is built, we use your submitted figures, mark them as provisional, and note what was assumed.
  • If a document isn't available in the form described, send what you have and tell us what it covers. A partial answer by the date is more useful than a complete one later.

How to Respond

Reply to Sarah Mitchell, quoting the reference (for example, RPS-1) against each item. Files can be sent in any format. Questions on any item can go to Daniel Vetsko at Kith Consulting, who is preparing the baseline.
