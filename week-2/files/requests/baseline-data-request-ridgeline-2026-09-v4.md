## Why This Matters

Your questionnaire notes a REIT client (about $4.5M of revenue) that intends to require property-manager emissions reporting by 2027, and your board is evaluating a sustainability services line. Both start with Ridgeline being able to state its own footprint accurately. At the moment the carbon template captures only part of it.

## What We Found in Your FY 2025 Submissions

We compared your carbon collection template with your climate questionnaire, figure by figure.

- Three template rows (Denver HQ, Phoenix, Salt Lake City) have an extra field, which pushes the fleet gasoline figure into the source column.
- Denver HQ gas is 8,500 therms in the template and 4,200 therms in the questionnaire. Phoenix electricity is 95,000 kWh in the template and about 62,000 kWh in the questionnaire.
- The Las Vegas office (opened January 2025) isn't in the template.
- The template holds about 10,600 gallons of fleet gasoline and no diesel; the questionnaire reports 22,400 gallons of gasoline and 4,800 gallons of diesel for 40 vehicles.
- Regional office electricity is labelled as utility bills in the template, while the questionnaire describes it as estimated from square footage.
