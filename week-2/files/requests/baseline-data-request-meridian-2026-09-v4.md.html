Why This Matters

Buyers' diligence teams increasingly include ESG data, and the first thing they test is whether a company's own submissions agree. Meridian's data is in good shape, but the two FY 2025 submissions differ in a few places. Resolving them now means there are no open questions in this area during the sale process.

What We Found in Your FY 2025 Submissions

We compared your carbon collection template with your climate questionnaire, figure by figure.

  • Electricity matches between the carbon template and the questionnaire at all 16 facilities. Gas matches at 12.
  • At Miami, Phoenix, Jacksonville DC and San Antonio DC, the template records zero gas; the questionnaire reports 12,500 therms in total.
  • The template records 3,200 gallons of fleet gasoline across five offices. The questionnaire states that Meridian does not operate a vehicle fleet.
  • Four facilities are sourced from accounting records, and Boston's electricity is a 60% allocation of the building total, with no methodology note.
  • Your organisation chart gives no site list, so we cannot yet check the template's 16 facilities against company records.

What You Have Already Provided

These are not requested again:

  • Electricity for all 16 facilities, consistent across both submissions.
  • Gas for 12 facilities, consistent across both submissions.

How We Will Use It

  • Your data is used for your company's baseline and Northwood's reporting only. It is not shared with other portfolio companies.
  • Where an item hasn't arrived by the time the baseline is built, we use your submitted figures, mark them as provisional, and note what was assumed.
  • If a document isn't available in the form described, send what you have and tell us what it covers. A partial answer by the date is more useful than a complete one later.
