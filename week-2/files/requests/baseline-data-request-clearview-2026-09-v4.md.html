Baseline Climate Data Request: Clearview Environmental Services

From: Sarah Mitchell, COO, Northwood Capital (prepared with Kith Consulting)
To: Steven Park (COO) and Greg Marshall (EHS Director)
Issue date: Monday, September 28, 2026 (planned)
Edition: Portfolio edition: Kith Climate cohort case, fictional client. The issue timetable is as planned in September 2026.
Status: As prepared for issue, September 2026

Why This Matters

Your questionnaire notes five ESG-scored RFPs in which Clearview estimates $2.0M to $3.0M was left on the table, and that 40% to 50% of revenue comes from clients with sustainability commitments. A confirmed facility-level baseline is the starting point for competing on those criteria, and for the board materials Northwood prepares.

What We Found in Your FY 2025 Submissions

We compared your carbon collection template with your climate questionnaire, figure by figure.

  • Your company totals for electricity, gas and fleet fuel match exactly across the carbon template and the questionnaire.
  • The template lists 12 facilities, including Richmond VA and Raleigh NC, and separate Indianapolis HQ and lab rows. The questionnaire lists 9 facilities, with Indianapolis as one combined site, and its utility list doesn't include Virginia or North Carolina.
  • Refrigerant is reported for one site only.
  • Your questionnaire reports a fleet of 85 vehicles, with GPS telematics on all 85. The Q4 2025 board deck lists 115 trucks and vehicles and 18 drilling rigs.

What You Have Already Provided

These are not requested again:

  • Fleet fuel (about 100,500 gallons of diesel and 55,400 gallons of gasoline) from fuel-card records. The two submissions agree on these volumes; which vehicles they cover is confirmed in the request below.
  • Insurance premium history for 2022 to 2025.

How We Will Use It

  • Your data is used for your company's baseline and Northwood's reporting only. It is not shared with other portfolio companies.
  • Where an item hasn't arrived by the time the baseline is built, we use your submitted figures, mark them as provisional, and note what was assumed.
  • If a document isn't available in the form described, send what you have and tell us what it covers. A partial answer by the date is more useful than a complete one later.

How to Respond

Reply to Sarah Mitchell, quoting the reference (for example, CES-1) against each item. Files can be sent in any format. Questions on any item can go to Daniel Vetsko at Kith Consulting, who is preparing the baseline.
