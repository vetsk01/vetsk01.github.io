Whether the six additional requests (Keystone, NovaCare, Patriot, Pinnacle, Ridgeline and Trident) go out on Monday or later is your decision.

Every ask across the eleven requests sits in one of three tiers, set by a single rule:

| Tier | Test | Due |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Blocks the Week 3 baseline | Without it, a baseline figure would rest on a known conflict or gap of 5.0% or more of a company's electricity, gas or fleet fuel, or on a figure implausible on its face at 5.0% or more of the footprint; or it blocks issuing a request | 5 business days |
| 2. Baseline proceeds provisionally | Resolving it changes figures by less than 5.0%, or changes source grade, traceability or scope classification | 10 business days |
| 3. Needed after Week 3 | No effect on Week 3 figures; informs Weeks 4 to 6 or the next collection cycle | 10 business days; partial answers accepted |

The tiers set sequence, not importance. The full list of 79 asks is in `finding-criticality-register-v3.csv`.

## If Answers Are Late

The Week 3 baseline still covers all 10 companies, on a stated provisional rule:
- Where the template is known to be wrong and the questionnaire gives a figure (for example NovaCare's two migrated clinics, Patriot's two zeroed branches and Meridian's four zero-gas sites), the questionnaire figure is used and flagged.
- Where the two submissions conflict and neither is known to be right, as at Harvest Ridge, a range is shown.
- Everything else uses the submitted template, marked provisional.

Each such figure carries a note stating what was assumed and what would close the gap. Late Tier 1 answers reduce confidence in specific figures, but they don't stop the baseline.
