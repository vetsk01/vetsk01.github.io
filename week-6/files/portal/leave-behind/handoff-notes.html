Judgment to carry

  • The absences are the finding. Ten of ten companies have no board oversight of climate, no dedicated climate role and no target. The evidence map's "none exists" state is an answered disclosure, not a gap (D-6-50).
  • The governance paragraph names the executive; it does not claim Northwood confirmed it. Keep that until DR6-01 is answered. Then write a new narrative version with the confirmed wording (the Governance section of narrative-v2.json), update the C-GOV-01 fund row text in the intake script, and run the nine steps; check Q09 reads the responsible executive from the settings.
  • Applicability is mostly "unknown", on purpose. Never turn unknown into no, and never state a legal conclusion on CSRD or California (charter R8).
  • Three bases, never mixed. Fund Scope 1 and 2 and the Scope 3 estimate are ownership-period totals; company rows are full-year; PCAF financed emissions use full-year company emissions times the attribution factor. Never add one to another.
  • Scope 3 is a grade C estimate, likely high. It rests on one month of costs, sector average pay and one supplier sector per company; Clearview's estimate carries the largest share. It rests on the USEEIO waste management and remediation sector factor (562000), which includes waste-disposal emissions, so it is likely high for subcontracted remediation. DR6-16 (supplier spend by category) is the way to replace it.
  • The physical-loss figure is gross, physical-only and as at 16 October 2026. Not a CY2025 figure, and not additive with Week 4's what-if lines. The by-fund split adds back to the total.
  • Week 4's "carbon" column is not a carbon cost. The board pages do not use it.

Traps

  • Run state before every gate check. A check leaves the state stale by design.
  • Do not edit frozen files. A change needs a new version, a decision, a re-run and a re-sign.
  • Downloads and PDFs need the owner's yes (D-6-43, D-6-48, D-6-49, D-6-54; the EPA factor file, J-064).
  • Technical notes for whoever re-runs the scripts (three environment traps):

  • The preview server cannot read OneDrive. Serve copies from a temporary folder.
