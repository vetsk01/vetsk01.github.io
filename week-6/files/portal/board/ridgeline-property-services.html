What we ask of management

  • whether anyone's pay is linked to energy or emissions results.
  • whether there is a plan to reduce emissions and cost, with an owner and dates.
  • where climate risk sits in the company's own risk review.
  • electricity contracts and certificates, so purchased power can be measured by what the company bought.
  • how much of the company's energy is renewable.
  • how much revenue comes from fossil-fuel customers.
  • what was actually spent on energy and climate measures in 2025 and what is committed for 2026.
  • whether the company uses an internal carbon price.
  • figures for emissions to water.
  • tonnes of hazardous waste and any permit breaches.
