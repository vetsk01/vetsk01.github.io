D2: master register, applicability screen and coverage-and-gap matrix (v2)

Record ids. D-6-n is a Week 6 decision, J-n a journal entry, F-0n a review finding, DR6-n a data request, A-n an assumption; all are in the engagement record.

Portfolio edition: Kith Climate cohort case, fictional client. In the case, this page is internal: it names companies. The LP report (D3) is the anonymised document. The v1 files in this folder are kept as frozen; use the v2 files.

How to read the numbers

  • Evidence map (352 cells): 98 covered, 42 none exists (the company has no board oversight, role or target, and that is the disclosure), 107 partial, 105 gap. Financed emissions (C-MET-09) are now covered for every company and the fund; Scope 3 (C-MET-04) is partial, because it is an estimate with categories combined. Gaps are mostly market-based Scope 2, remuneration, internal carbon price and the environment lines.
  • Engine status (filled-register): 14 of 32 concepts read covered. The engine counts a narrative concept only when the LP report addresses it, and a figure concept only when the data table has a row for every company.
  • Applicability screen: 0 yes, 82 unknown, 12 monitor, 10 no. The ten 'no' answers are SB 261 for each company, whose revenue is below the USD 500M test on its own. Everything else is unknown because the data room lacks the entity facts; the screen never turns a missing fact into a 'no'. The legal questions behind the unknowns are with Northwood and its counsel (A-14, D-6-60) and are not answered here (charter R8).
  • DR6-16 is new: supplier spend by category and headcount including contractors, to replace the spend-based Scope 3 estimate.

How to re-run (no Kith)

  1. Update the inputs named in the manifest (carbon submissions, questionnaires, spine, financials) and the questionnaire reads in my-work/week-6/pipeline/questionnaire-reads-v1.csv.
  2. Run the steps in my-work/week-6/fund-level/rerun-note-v2.md up to my-work/week-6/pipeline/build-d2-v1.py.
  3. Judgments live in my-work/week-6/pipeline/evidence-rules-v2.csv (one row per concept: the state and evidence for companies and for the fund, with the basis). Change a rule only with a recorded reason; a rule's state is overridden by data-table rows where they exist.
  4. Applicability rules are in my-work/week-6/fund-level/library/applicability/applicability-rules-v2.csv; framework facts and their verification are in library/standards/ and the verification log.

Dates

The report date is the manifest setting (not typed anywhere). Data period is calendar 2025; revenue and the Scope 3 spend are LTM to 31 January 2026 (Week 3 rule R4). Each framework row carries its retrieval date; none is flagged stale unless its re-check date has passed.
