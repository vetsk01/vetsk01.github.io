06Basis

How to read this page

Colour is what to do

Mitigate now. Likely and large. Capital and management time go here first.
Hedge or insure. Unlikely but large. Transfer the risk: insurance, hedges, contract terms.
Accept and monitor. Likely but small. Watch the trend; do not spend capital on it yet.
Deprioritize. Unlikely and small. Note it; revisit once a year.

Card colour is the type

Earns today. Revenue the company already books. Counted at exit.
Revenue not yet won. Not in earnings. Never counted at exit.
Efficiency measure. An energy or cost saving.
Not yet sized. No cost or saving stated yet.
Company plan. A growth plan the company has, not in earnings.

Grade key

AOfficial published data, or an actual from a system of record.
BA figure the company reported, an accounting estimate, or a value derived from A and B inputs.
CJudgment, a proxy, undated or simulated data. A Kith estimate: no outside source or company has confirmed it. Marked †.

Method and caveats
  • Report date October 9, 2026. Impact is the effect on adjusted EBITDA in the year it occurs, against EBITDA for the twelve months to January 31, 2026 (data-room date). Modelled exit is the report date plus the remaining hold. It is Kith's model, not a Northwood plan: where the hold is a floor of one year (the window has passed or no date is stated) it is the earliest modelled date. Where Northwood's own exit plan differs, use that.
  • Large and likely follow Northwood's own scale at their defaults: High impact from 15 percent of EBITDA, Likely from a 35 percent chance over the hold. The sliders only change how points are classed; they change no dollar figure.
  • The exit-year control selects which companies are in the book (those exiting by that year). Totals are sums of company totals from the frozen model; carbon price is left out because it overlaps the energy line, and lines within a company are not independent.
  • Cost to respond to customer data requests is a Kith estimate, grade C, from two secondary benchmark pages and no independent project-fee check. Its value protected is an upper what-if: the whole exposed revenue treated as lost, not likelihood-weighted; the half is Kith's illustration.
  • Revenue not yet won means revenue the company has not yet won; it is not in LTM earnings and is not capitalised through the exit multiple. Earns today is revenue the company already books.
  • Efficiency measures are rated on the low end of savings and the high end of cost. The exit value effect is the annual saving times the company's exit multiple; whether a buyer pays that multiple on a saving only months old is the open question.
  • Dates that a company's file plans for an event (a contract renewal, a board pilot) are read on the data room's own clock, about January 31, 2026, and are shown as planned in the company's file; they are not tested against the report date. An event is treated as past only where it was already past on that clock.
  • Two small errors in the frozen data text are corrected on this page (a questionnaire line cite and a truncated clause). They are listed in the corrections file, and the data layer carries the earlier text until its next version.
  • The physical-weather lines of Keystone, NovaCare, Patriot, Pinnacle, Ridgeline and Trident were re-derived by an independent check script; the other lines are model-run. Derived energy is a low case (gas at the industrial price).
  • Where the data room gave no figure, the line is estimated and marked †: insurance premiums (the portfolio's observed premium ratio, a peer ratio the owner allowed, and the US casualty rate change), supplier concentration (a McKinsey Global Institute disruption benchmark scaled by a stated dependence factor), two customer data requirement lines derived from the company's own statements and customer counts, the Harvest Ridge water lines, and six efficiency measures sized from the DOE industrial assessment database. Each is cross-checked in the table on the exposure model page.
  • Patriot Staffing's files give two exit timings. The model uses the deal team's base case, the first half of 2029, because the 2027 to 2028 target needs EBITDA of $32M to $38M against $23.8M today, the add-on pipeline is paused, and the plan itself says the hold may run to Year 5 to 6.
  • Harvest Ridge's drought curtailment line rests in part on simulated plant values, by a specific exception to the rule that keeps simulated data out of other figures; the water price line uses only the company's reported 28 million gallons. The 2025 pressure drops, which the water authority attributed to demand and maintenance, are already the impact event in the weather line, so the two lines overlap in cause.

† Kith estimate (grade C, proxy data): the input is a benchmark, a judgment or an inference that no outside source or company has confirmed. The workings were scripted and checked in the build and passed the project's review gates; the input itself was not verified against a third-party source or a project quote. Treat it as an order of magnitude, not a quote. The data room's own figures carry grade A or B.
