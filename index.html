How I built it

Alongside the client work I built a way of working that makes AI-produced analysis checkable: standing instructions for the AI, read-only reviewer agents, rules written before the results, checks that must prove they can fail, and gates that freeze what was signed. The capstone tells how that grew from no review in Week 1 to gated, independently checked delivery by Week 6.

About this portfolio

This is my work from the Climate Work Residency, a six-week programme run by Kith Climate. Northwood Capital and its ten portfolio companies are a fictional case, and the data room is Kith Climate's; the analysis, the deliverables and the way they were built are mine, made with Claude Code.
