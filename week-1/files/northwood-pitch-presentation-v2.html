What AI-Native Actually Buys You

Why AI-native changes the prioritization math

Traditional consulting often prices depth per company — every additional portfolio company is a new fixed cost. A Claude Code-native build prices depth once, in the methodology. After that, extending it reuses what is already built instead of rebuilding it.

Traditional model

Depth is priced per company. Each additional portco means new hours, new templates rebuilt from scratch, new context-loading for the firm.

AI-native model

Depth is priced once, in the build. CLAUDE.md (the project's standing instructions file), the reference architecture, and the calculation logic are the methodology — applying them to a new company is templated, not re-derived.

You asked us to recommend where to focus — that's still right. What changes is what happens to the other six.

Six weeks builds the methodology on the highest-signal cohort. Extending it to the remaining six becomes a templated next step, not a from-scratch engagement.
